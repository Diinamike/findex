implements a basic text widget which has a vertical scroll bar configured to do
the “right thing.”  Using the ScrolledText class is a lot easier than
setting up a text widget and scroll bar directly. The text widget and scrollbar are packed together in a Frame ,
and the methods of the Pack , Grid and Place geometry managers are acquired from the Frame object.
This allows the ScrolledText widget to be used directly to achieve
most normal geometry management behavior. Should more specific control be necessary, the following attributes are
available: class tkinter.scrolledtext. ScrolledText ( master = None , ** kw ) ¶ frame ¶ The frame which surrounds the text and scroll bar widgets. vbar ¶ The scroll bar widget.
