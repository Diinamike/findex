equivalent Unicode character(s), e.g. html5['gt;'] == '>' .
Note that the trailing semicolon is included in the name (e.g. 'gt;' ),
however some of the names are accepted by the standard even without the
semicolon: in this case the name is present with and without the ';' .
See also html.unescape() . Added in version 3.3. html.entities. entitydefs ¶ A dictionary mapping XHTML 1.0 entity definitions to their replacement text in
ISO Latin-1. html.entities. name2codepoint ¶ A dictionary that maps HTML4 entity names to the Unicode code points. html.entities. codepoint2name ¶ A dictionary that maps Unicode code points to HTML4 entity names.
